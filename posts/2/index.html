We're proud to share that Kheshig took 1st place at SAS CTF 2026 Quals, the qualifier for Kaspersky's Security Analyst Summit. We joined our Kazakh brothers at FR13NDS TEAM and played as FR13NDS x Kheshig - the joint team solved 20 of 24 challenges for 4377 points; Kheshig landed 17 of them.

We'll be up against some of the best teams around: ARESxTRX (@ARESxCTF and @TheRomanXpl0it teamed up), BunkyoWesterns, r3kapig, and thehackerscrew. It's going to be tough, and we can't wait. See you in Bali.
